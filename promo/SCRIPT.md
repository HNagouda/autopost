# Voiceover script

These are the lines as recorded, transcribed from the takes in `voiceover/` with Whisper, and the times they play in `out/autopost-promo-4k-vo.mp4`, which runs 2:01. Each take is numbered in script order (`1.mp3` to `12.mp3`) and starts half a second into its scene. To re-record a line, replace its take, run `python3 scripts/clean_voiceover.py`, and render again. If the new take is longer, its scene in `src/features.ts` or `src/scenes/` needs to grow by the same amount.
